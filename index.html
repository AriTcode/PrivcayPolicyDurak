Privacy Policy for Durak

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Durak does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information.

  • No account is needed and none is created.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party code.
  • No network use. The app works fully offline and makes no network connections.
  • Saved on your device only: your settings (house rules, bot levels, sound, music and appearance) and your statistics (how often you were safe or the fool). This data stays on your device. It is not sent anywhere and is removed when you delete the app.
  • Names you type (your own name and the names of players in pass-and-play) are used only inside the game on your device.
  • Children: the app has no chat, no user-generated content, no purchases and no links out, and is suitable for all ages.

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
